About Ballpark

Ballpark is a home for quick guessing games about the world. The first one is Sort Of: rank five things by a hidden stat and see how close you get.

Everyone has a gut sense for how things compare. Which costs more to make, a nickel or a dime? Which has more teeth, a dog or a person? Which Monopoly property is cheaper, Electric Company or Virginia Avenue? Ballpark puts that gut sense to the test, then shows you the real numbers and a fact worth knowing.

How it works

There's a new Daily puzzle every day, the same for everyone, plus Unlimited puzzles across categories like Food & Drink, Games, Sports, Animals & Body, Space, Around the US, Earth & Nature, Movies, TV & Books, Brands & Tech, Grab Bag, Timelines. Rounds take about a minute. Past Dailies, with their answers and sources, live in the archive.

Accuracy

Every puzzle is built from published data, and each answer names its source. If you think a number is wrong, please tell us which puzzle it was and we'll check it.

What's next

More puzzles and categories are on the way, plus a new game, Find It: spot the bunny, the sasquatch or whoever is hiding, as fast as you can.

Ballpark is an independent project and isn't affiliated with any brand, team, game, book or film mentioned in its puzzles. Names are used only to state facts about them.

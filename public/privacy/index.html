Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Ballpark is built to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what happens when you play.

What we store

We don't ask for your name, email or an account. Your scores, streak and settings are saved in your own browser on your own device (using your browser's local storage). We can't see them, and they never leave your device. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Moving stats to another device

If you use Move my stats, your stats are packed into a link or QR code that you open on your other device. The stats travel inside that link only. They aren't uploaded to or stored on our servers. Anyone you give the link to could see your stats, so only open it on your own devices.

Site analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. These numbers are totals and are not used to identify you.

Advertising

Ballpark may show ads from Google AdSense to help pay for the site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Children

Ballpark is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email ballpark.contact@proton.me.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Ballpark is built to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what happens when you play.

What we store

You can play without an account. Your scores, streak and settings are saved in your own browser on your own device (using your browser's local storage). Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Optional accounts

If you choose to create an account to sync your stats across devices, we store your email address, a securely scrambled (hashed) version of your password, and your game stats (scores, games played and which Dailies you finished). We never see or store your actual password. We use your email only to run your account and to answer messages you send us. We don't sell it or use it for marketing.

Signing in sets one cookie that keeps you signed in. It's only used for your account, not for ads or tracking.

You can delete your account at any time from the account menu (the person icon at the top of the game). That permanently deletes your email, password and synced stats from our servers. Stats saved in your browser stay until you clear them.

How we protect it

Account data is stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare, and is only sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Passwords are hashed with a unique salt, sign-in sessions use a secure cookie that page scripts can't read, and repeated failed sign-ins are slowed down and locked. To limit abuse, we briefly keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your network address when you sign up or sign in. It can't be turned back into your address and is cleared automatically. We never store payment information, because there's nothing to buy.

How long we keep it

We keep account data until you delete your account. Browser data stays on your device until you clear it.

Site analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. These numbers are totals and are not used to identify you.

Advertising

Ballpark may show ads from Google AdSense to help pay for the site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Children

Ballpark is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13. Anyone can play without an account, but accounts are only for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

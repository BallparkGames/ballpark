How to play Batting Order

You get five things. They're ranked by a hidden stat. Drag them into order from most (top) to least (bottom), then lock it in.

  • Daily: one puzzle a day, same for everyone. You get a clue.
  • Easy: you see the stat before you rank.
  • Normal: you get a clue. You can show a hint for 25 points.

Scoring: 100 points if your #1 is right, 75 for each other spot you get exactly right, and 25 for anything just one spot off. 400 is perfect.

Can't drag? Tap one card, then another, to swap them. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys on a card.
